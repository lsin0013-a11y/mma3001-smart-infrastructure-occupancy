# G.20 ventilation strategies: 2026 test period

Outdoor air at 10 L/s per person; CO2 simulated minute by minute (well-mixed room, 810 m³, 0.0052 L/s CO2 per person, 420 ppm outdoors; steady state at the design rate 940 ppm; background infiltration 0.3 air changes per hour). All CO2 values are modelled, not measured. Sensor outages excluded from scoring. *Air vs fixed* is outdoor air supplied relative to the fixed schedule (a proxy for fan and conditioning energy).

Generated by `python scripts/ventilation_study.py --room G.20`.

Design occupancy (fixed strategies and fallback): **42 people** (420 L/s), the 99.9% percentile of the headcount over occupied minutes in the training period (before 2026-01-01).

## Sensor failure: test period with the sensor dead throughout

| Case | Air (m³) | Person-hours > limit | Peak (ppm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working sensor (live) | 90,242 | 0.0 | 861 |
| Dead sensor, no fallback | 0 | 148.3 | 1780 |
| Dead sensor, average headcount | 79,161 | 133.4 | 1703 |
| Dead sensor, fallback (design air when average > 0.25) | 2,205,126 | 0.0 | 915 |

Sensor outages covered 0.9% of the training period. At that rate the expected air use with the fallback is about 109,886 m³ (+22% on the working sensor alone).
