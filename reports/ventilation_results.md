# G.20 ventilation strategies: 2026 test period

Outdoor air at 10 L/s per person; CO2 simulated minute by minute (well-mixed room, 81 m³, 0.0052 L/s CO2 per person, 420 ppm outdoors; steady state at the design rate 940 ppm; background infiltration 0.3 air changes per hour). All CO2 values are modelled, not measured. Sensor outages excluded from scoring. *Air vs fixed* is outdoor air supplied relative to the fixed schedule (a proxy for fan and conditioning energy).

Generated by `python scripts/ventilation_study.py --room G.20`.

## Safety margin, chosen on 2025 (live sensor, 5-min control)

Smallest margin that cuts person-hours above the limit by at least 90%: **15%** (11.5 L/s per measured person).
